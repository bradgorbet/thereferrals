The band

Highly recommended by people who were there.

The Referrals play the songs from the mixtapes, the dorm rooms and the car radio, start to finish, with the volume where it belongs. Grunge and Britpop, college radio and new wave, plus a few newer songs that earned a spot on the tape.

Right now we're a three-piece: vocals and guitar, bass, and drums. We're always open to new folks joining in. If you know these songs and want to play, get in touch.

We only play fundraisers, for charities and causes we believe in. Every show is a benefit, and most of them come the way our name suggests: someone saw us, then told a friend about a cause that needed a night out. Add the charities you've played for
